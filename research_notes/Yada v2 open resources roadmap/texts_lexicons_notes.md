# Open Bible texts, dictionaries, lexicons, translation helps and textual-variant data for Yada v2

Researched 2026-10-11. "Primary" = licence text read from a LICENSE file, metadata file, README or the publisher's own licence page during this session. Anything else is marked UNVERIFIED. No resource found here carries an AI-use restriction in its licence text, except where noted (NET: unclear). The CC licences (BY, BY-SA, CC0) have no AI clause. Yada's own rule 9 (licensed translations never enter AI calls) applies to anything not openly licensed, which here means NET.

## More open English translations

### Takeaway
The open English translations that are modern, readable and commercially usable are Open English Bible (CC0 / public domain, NT complete but OT partial), Free Bible Version (CC BY-SA 4.0), unfoldingWord ULT (literal, CC BY-SA 4.0) and UST (plain-language, CC BY-SA 4.0), Translation for Translators (very plain, CC BY-SA 4.0), and World Messianic Bible (public domain, a WEB variant). Berean Standard Bible and Berean Majority Bible are public domain since 30 April 2023. The NET Bible is not open: it is copyright Biblical Studies Press, and commercial use goes through HarperCollins, so Yada should treat it like NIV/NKJV (display-only, licensed).

The NT is an edit of the public-domain Twentieth Century New Testament (Westcott-Hort base). The OT is being built from public-domain translations by McFadyen, Kent and Moffatt. The repo separates `release` books from `development` (partial or rough) books, so the OT is incomplete. Last push to the repo was 2026-07-20. — [OEB GitHub README](https://github.com/openenglishbible/Open-English-Bible)

### Inferences
- Best additions for ordinary readers: the UST (plain, meaning-based, CC BY-SA, actively maintained) as a "simple" reading companion, and the FBV (modern idiom, with audio). The OEB is attractive because it is CC0, but its OT gaps make it a poor main text. T4T is very expansive and reads as translator scaffolding, not a reading Bible.
- Under CC BY-SA, shipping the text inside an app is fine commercially. Yada's own edits or formatting of the text must stay BY-SA. The share-alike duty attaches to the adapted text, not to Yada's app code.
- unfoldingWord's trademark clause matters in the UI. If Yada reformats or edits the ULT/UST in a way that counts as "modifying", the unfoldingWord® name must come off. Display the text unmodified, or label it neutrally (for example "Literal Text, adapted from unfoldingWord ULT").
- NET's study notes (60,000+) are not open. The NET should be treated as a licensed, display-only translation under rule 9, and it is probably not worth pursuing.

## Bible dictionaries and encyclopedias

### Takeaway
The strongest modern, readable, commercially usable dictionaries are under CC BY-SA 4.0: the Tyndale Open Bible Dictionary and its multilingual adaptation, the Aquifer Open Bible Dictionary (6,130 English articles). Bible Aquifer (Mission Mutual) also has several more CC BY-SA reference works that ship as JSON/Markdown on GitHub: the UBS Greek and Hebrew dictionaries (Louw-Nida lineage), the Dictionary of Bible Themes, UBS flora, fauna and realia lexicons, Biblica study notes, SIL Open Translator's Notes, maps and images. The public-domain 19th-century dictionaries (Easton's, Smith's, ISBE 1915, Hitchcock's) are free of obligations but dated in content and prose.

The page states: "This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License." The notice sits after both downloads and does not say whether it covers each separately. No AI or commercial restriction is mentioned. — [tyndaleopenresources.com](https://tyndaleopenresources.com/)
- **Aquifer Open Bible Dictionary**: "This work is an adaptation of Tyndale Open Bible Dictionary © 2023 Tyndale House Publishers, licensed under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license. The adaptation … was created by Mission Mutual and is also licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0." It is available in English, Tok Pisin, Arabic, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swahili and Simplified Chinese, as JSON and Markdown per language. — [AquiferOpenBibleDictionary README](https://github.com/BibleAquifer/AquiferOpenBibleDictionary)

### Inferences
- The Aquifer Open Bible Dictionary is the Tyndale dictionary plus maintenance, translations and a clean JSON/Markdown format. Taking it from Aquifer may beat taking the Tyndale zip, but attribution must then name both Tyndale House Publishers and Mission Mutual, with the adaptation notice.
- The UBS Greek and Hebrew dictionaries are Louw-Nida semantic-domain lexicons, a rare and valuable thing to find openly licensed. They are better for "what does this word mean here" than Strong's.
- For lay readers: the Tyndale/Aquifer dictionary is written in modern evangelical-reference prose and suits non-experts. The Dictionary of Bible Themes is a topical index and good for "find more on this theme". The 19th-century PD dictionaries carry outdated archaeology and quaint language, so they are a poor fit for Yada's tone.
- All of these are offline-friendly: static JSON/Markdown, thousands of entries, likely tens of MB in total (size UNVERIFIED).

### Gaps
- Download sizes for the Tyndale zips and Aquifer repos were not measured.
- Whether the Tyndale Open Bible Dictionary zip carries its own licence header was not checked (the zip was not downloaded).
- Licence fields for the UBS Fauna and Realia lexicons and for Aquifer Open Study Notes were not individually read.

## Open lexicons

### Takeaway
Commercially usable lexicons: Abbott-Smith (PD, TEI-marked), Dodson (PD), BDB (CC0 edition on Aquifer), Strong's (PD by age), LSJ (Perseus TEI under CC BY-SA 4.0), STEPBible TBESG/TBESH/TFLSJ (CC BY 4.0; Yada already has TBESG/TBESH), and the UBS Louw-Nida-based dictionaries (CC BY-SA 4.0). The unfoldingWord Greek Lexicon (UGL) exists, but its licence could not be confirmed from a file.

### Cited Findings
- **Abbott-Smith**, A Manual Greek Lexicon of the New Testament (1922): "The lexicon (abbott-smith.tei.xml), including the marked up version in this repository, is in the public domain." Markup and checking are complete. A SWORD module exists. Caveat: "Certain restrictions apply" to the archive.org source PDF (not to the XML). — [Abbott-Smith README](https://github.com/translatable-exegetical-tools/Abbott-Smith)
- **Dodson Greek Lexicon**: "This lexicon, in all of its forms, is in the public domain." — [Dodson README](https://github.com/biblicalhumanities/Dodson-Greek-Lexicon)
- **BDB Hebrew Lexicon** (Aquifer edition, 2,277 entries) and BDB Aramaic: "This edition of the Brown-Driver-Briggs Hebrew Lexicon is licensed with a CC0 license and is in the public domain." — [BDBHebrewLexicon metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BibleAquifer/BDBHebrewLexicon/main/eng/metadata.json)
- **LSJ** (Perseus lexica repo): "Unless otherwise indicated, all contents of this repository are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License. You must offer Perseus any modifications you make." The README also says Perseus materials "are provided for the personal use of students, scholars, and the public" and that "Materials within the Perseus DL have varying copyright status". These two statements sit uneasily together; check per file. — [PerseusDL/lexica README](https://github.com/PerseusDL/lexica)
- **STEPBible TFLSJ**: "Full LSJ entries for all Bible words (NT, LXX, Apoc & variants), formatted for easy reading … linked to extended Strongs". **TBESG**: "Brief definitions for all Greek Bible words … using corrected Abbott-Smith when available". The repository licence is "CC BY 4.0". The repo also has TIPNR (proper names with all references), TVTMS (versification) and TEHMC/TEGMC (morphology code expansions). — [STEPBible-Data README](https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data)
- **unfoldingWord Greek Lexicon (en_ugl)** exists on Door43 (description "unfoldingWord® Greek Lexicon", last updated 2025-08-29). No LICENSE.md was found at the master branch path; the licence is UNVERIFIED (believed CC BY-SA 4.0 like other uW resources). en_uhal (Hebrew/Aramaic lexicon) had no LICENSE.md at the probed paths; UNVERIFIED. — [Door43 repo search](https://git.door43.org/api/v1/repos/search?q=en_ugl&limit=5)
- **Strong's** (1890): public domain by age; no primary file checked (UNVERIFIED source file).

### Inferences
- Yada already has TBESG, which embeds corrected Abbott-Smith. Adding raw Abbott-Smith adds little. TFLSJ adds full LSJ entries (scholarly, dense) and is CC BY 4.0 with no share-alike, so it is easier to ship than Perseus LSJ (BY-SA plus an "offer Perseus modifications" request).
- For lay readers, the UBS semantic dictionaries (contextual senses) are the most useful upgrade. BDB and LSJ are expert-level.

### Gaps
- The en_ugl and en_uhal licence files were not located. UNVERIFIED.
- The coverage and completeness of the UGL were not checked.

## Translation helps (unfoldingWord tN, tW, tA, tQ and similar)

### Takeaway
All four unfoldingWord helps are CC BY-SA 4.0 (confirmed from LICENSE.md, © 2026, so actively maintained), with a trademark-removal clause for derivatives. They are written for Bible translators, not readers. tW (key-term articles) and tQ (comprehension questions) carry over best to lay readers. tN is huge (87,573 notes) and much of it is translation advice.

### Cited Findings
- **tN, tW, tA, tQ** LICENSE.md, each with the same pattern: "Copyright © 2026 by unfoldingWord. This work is made available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License." The unfoldingWord name and logo need written permission. Unmodified copies keep the ® mark. Derivatives "must remove the unfoldingWord® trademark", "indicate what changes you have made", attribute as "The original work by unfoldingWord is available from unfoldingword.org/utn" (or /utw, /uta, /utq), and stay CC BY-SA. — [en_tn LICENSE](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_tn/raw/branch/master/LICENSE.md); [en_tw LICENSE](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_tw/raw/branch/master/LICENSE.md); [en_ta LICENSE](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_ta/raw/branch/master/LICENSE.md); [en_tq LICENSE](https://git.door43.org/unfoldingWord/en_tq/raw/branch/master/LICENSE.md)
- Aquifer mirrors these with entry counts: UWTranslationNotes 87,573, UWTranslationWords 953, plus UWTranslationQuestions, UWTranslationManual (tA) and UWOpenBibleStories. All were pushed 2026-09-29. — [UWTranslationNotes metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BibleAquifer/UWTranslationNotes/main/eng/metadata.json); [UWTranslationWords metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BibleAquifer/UWTranslationWords/main/eng/metadata.json); [BibleAquifer org](https://github.com/BibleAquifer)
- **SIL Open Translator's Notes** (9,011 entries): "© 2024 SIL International", CC BY-SA 4.0, source opentn.bible. — [SILOpenTranslatorsNotes metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BibleAquifer/SILOpenTranslatorsNotes/main/eng/metadata.json)
- Aquifer also lists FIA Translation Guide, FIA Key Terms, AFBRT Translation Notes and PSLE (licences not read). — [BibleAquifer org](https://github.com/BibleAquifer)

### Inferences
- tN notes are keyed to ULT Greek and Hebrew quotes. Many explain figures of speech ("this is a metonymy…") and give alternate renderings, which can help a curious reader but is jargon-heavy. tA explains the figure-of-speech terms that tN links to.
- For Yada, tW (953 plain-English articles on key terms such as "grace" and "covenant") is the most reader-friendly. It is also grounding-eligible for Yada's pipeline, since BY-SA has no AI restriction. Any generated output derived from it may itself become BY-SA; that question needs a decision.
- Trademark: if Yada adapts the text (strips markup, edits), it must drop the unfoldingWord® name and use the "original work … available from" line instead.

### Gaps
- No readability study of tN or tW for lay audiences was found.
- The exact JSON sizes of the Aquifer mirrors were not measured.

## Textual variants and manuscripts

### Takeaway
Two openly licensed, commercially usable sources show where manuscripts and editions differ. The **SBLGNT apparatus** (CC BY 4.0 since the 2022 relicence; on GitHub as text and XML per book) compares SBLGNT with WH, Tregelles, NA28/NIV and Robinson-Pierpont. **CNTR** provides the Statistical Restoration GNT with a "Universal Apparatus" (CC BY 4.0) and raw manuscript transcriptions (CC BY-SA 4.0). STEPBible TAGNT, which Yada already has, already marks every word by edition (NA27/28, TR, SBLGNT, Treg, Byz, WH, THGNT). The THGNT text itself is not openly licensed.

### Cited Findings
- **SBLGNT** README: "The SBLGNT is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. Copyright 2010 by the Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software." Version history: v1.1 2022-12-19 "Update public version and license on github"; v1.2 2023-07-10 "Add Jn 7:53-8:11 (Pericope Adulturae)". The LICENSE file is the full CC BY 4.0 legal code, and sblgnt.com/license shows the same CC BY 4.0 text. — [SBLGNT README](https://github.com/LogosBible/SBLGNT); [SBLGNT LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LogosBible/SBLGNT/master/LICENSE); [sblgnt.com/license](https://sblgnt.com/license/)
- The repo holds `data/sblgntapp/text/*.txt` and `data/sblgntapp/xml` (the apparatus). A sample entry (Mark 1:1): "χριστοῦ WH ] + υἱοῦ θεοῦ Treg NA28; υἱοῦ τοῦ θεοῦ RP". The repo now redirects to Faithlife/SBLGNT. — [SBLGNT apparatus Mark.txt](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LogosBible/SBLGNT/master/data/sblgntapp/text/Mark.txt)
- **CNTR** resources page: "Greek New Testaments … copyright © by Alan Bunning released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0). Attribution must be given to Alan Bunning and the Center for New Testament Restoration in any derivative works, and any changes made must be indicated." This covers the Statistical Restoration (SR, "with the Universal Apparatus"), the Bunning Heuristic Prototype (BHP), and the King James Textus Receptus (KJTR). "Data … (CC BY-SA 4.0)" covers the CNTR Transcriptions (manuscript transcriptions) and the KoineGreek font. — [greekcntr.org/resources](https://greekcntr.org/resources/)
- SR README: "Copyright © 2022-2023 by Alan Bunning. All rights reserved. Released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)." It describes the SR as "the first computer-generated text derived directly from the earliest manuscript witnesses". — [CNTR SR GitHub](https://github.com/Center-for-New-Testament-Restoration/SR)
- A 2016 post said CNTR transcriptions were "a work in progress, so derivative works will not be allowed until later". The current resources page lists them under plain CC BY-SA 4.0, so the current page supersedes that statement. — [WebSearch summary of blog.greek-language.com](https://blog.greek-language.com/category/textual-criticism/); [greekcntr.org/resources](https://greekcntr.org/resources/)
- **STEPBible TAGNT**: "Greek text that includes all the words in NA27/28, TR and other major editions (SBLGNT, Treg, Byz, WH, THGNT). Each word is marked with the editions that contain it, positional variants, and meaning variants." The repo also mentions TOTMM ("Translators OT Manuscripts and Meanings"), and TAHOT includes "LXX additions included as Hebrew from BHS/BHK apparatus". Licence CC BY 4.0. — [STEPBible-Data README](https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data)
- **THGNT**: the YouVersion listing says © 2017 Tyndale House, Cambridge, used by permission of Crossway. No open licence was found, and a probe of github.com/Tyndale/THGNT returned 404. — [bible.com versions/2270](https://www.bible.com/versions/2270) (via WebSearch); [WebSearch summary](https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data)
- **OpenText**: not researched this session (gap).

### Inferences
- The cheapest reader-facing feature is already in hand. TAGNT's per-word edition flags let Yada show "missing from older manuscripts" (for example Mark 16:9-20, John 7:53-8:11, 1 John 5:7 KJV-only words) with no new data. The SBLGNT apparatus adds a compact, human-readable "which editions read what" line per variant unit.
- CNTR transcriptions (BY-SA) are expert-level, but they could support a "what the earliest manuscripts say" view. The share-alike applies only to the data Yada derives from them.
- Rule 4 holds: show only what the apparatus or TAGNT actually states. Do not paraphrase manuscript evidence beyond the source.

- The CNTR SR "Universal Apparatus" format and size were not inspected.
- Whether the THGNT apparatus is included in any open STEPBible file was not checked (TAGNT includes THGNT readings as flags only).
- OpenText.org licence and status were not researched.
