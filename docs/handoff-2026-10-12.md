  - Widgets and Live Activities show only public-domain text.

## Licensed translations: what applies

- API.Bible Starter covers non-commercial use only, never altered and never used with AI. §9.1 also forbids
  reading copyrighted text aloud (TTS) without the rights holder's written consent, so NIV, NKJV and NLT stay
  silent in the app.
- **NIV caution.** Biblica's express licence through api.bible requires a product with no AI or machine-learning
  features. Yada's explanations are AI-generated, though never from the NIV. So until Biblica or Hodder answers,
  NIV stays out of tester builds, and the proxy serves NKJV and NLT only.
- **The proxy is live** at https://yada-licensed-proxy.creotok.workers.dev. John deployed it on 12 Oct 2026, and
  it was checked the same day: NKJV and NLT return 200 with their copyright notice and a 30-day private cache; NIV,
  other paths, query strings and unknown books return 404; observability and logs are off in `wrangler.toml`.
  Build with `EXPO_PUBLIC_LICENSED_PROXY_URL=https://yada-licensed-proxy.creotok.workers.dev`. Before external
  testers get that build:
  - the privacy line, with the Cloudflare sentence, is approved and live on R2's privacy.html (12 Oct 2026);
  - update the R2-only sentence in `docs/app-store-listing.md`;
  - run the PR gates over `services/licensed-proxy`.
- **Two calls made on 12 Oct:**
  - VoiceOver may read licensed verses: that is the reader's own screen reader, not app speech.
  - Copy and Share keep working on licensed verses, but always append the translation name and its copyright line.
- No FUMS reporting for the native app (docs.api.bible fair-use page). A future website showing these texts would
  need it.
