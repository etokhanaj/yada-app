# Design review, 12 Oct 2026: slop, wireframe, undesigned states, magic moments, haptics

Four reviewers did a second slop pass over 261 frames (the v2, guide and next pages, each in Light, Dark and AX5).
A fifth looked at the flows (wireframe pass) and magic moments. A sixth read `apps/mobile` for states nobody designed,
and for haptics. Mechanical fixes are on the canvas. Everything below needs a decision or a frame.

## 1. Decisions for John

1. **Approved lines that repeat on screen.**
   - Psalm 51's "Written when Nathan the prophet came to David…" sits right above the BSB superscription that says the
     same thing (JR-01, JR-02; JR-03 shows it a third time).
   - Isaiah 40's plain section name "Comfort for the exiles in Babylon" is followed at once by the setting line "Words
     of comfort for the exiles in Babylon." (JR-04).
   - Proposal: show "Written when" only when the superscription isn't visible (About this psalm, guide, summaries).
     Show the setting line only where the section name isn't (the guide's In short).
2. **The verse-sheet header and source labels.** Your spec has "John 3:16 · Understand" and section labels with
   source codes ("In plain words · UST", "Readers differ · tN").
   - Reviewers flagged "Understand" as a feature name used as a title, and "UST" and "tN" as jargon.
   - NA frames use "1 Corinthians 14:34" with "passage · range" under it.
   - Proposal: keep the codes in Sources, and give the headings the plain source name only where two sources share a
     slot.
3. **Superseded frames.** The canvas still holds older versions of first launch, Understand (Yada-written "What's
   happening"), the guide (full passage text with Next) and Home ("All passages in John"). Whoever builds from it could
   build the wrong one. Proposal: move superseded frames to an "Archive" page. Current, per IA v3: VS, NA, JR, IA-11–14
   and ST.
4. **One name each.**
   - One translation is called "UST", "Simplified Text" and "Plain English".
   - "Key words" means English terms in DI-01 and Greek words in UX-10.
   - The passage guide has four names.
   - The lower half of Understand has three names: "Go deeper", "More" and "More on this verse".

## 2. Wireframe pass: structural issues, ranked by impact on a first-time reader

1. **"Passage finished" has no trigger now.**
   - The Home lead turning to Next (ON-07), the one-time reminder ask (NT-01), "2 of 46 passages read" (IA-14) and plan
     ticks (RP-03) all depend on it.
   - The old trigger was Next at the end of the guide's text. The current guide (NA-04) has no text and no Next.
   - **Design:** finished means reading or listening past the passage's last verse in the reader. The Next line and the
     one-time reminder ask live there (see magic moment 1).
2. **First launch puts a sheet before the first verse** (ON-01 → ON-02 guide sheet → × → hint → Understand). Every
   relaunch lands on Home.
   - **Design:** Start here opens the reader with the guide's two "Where we are" lines above verse 1 (the JR-02 and
     TY-03 pattern). Relaunch returns to the place left.
3. **Sheet anatomy differs between sheets.**
   - Headers differ (VS, UX-01, NA).
   - Back ‹ is unlabelled in some sheets and "‹ 14:34" in others.
   - The full player closes with ⌄ while everything else uses ×.
   - "Back to reading" survives only in PG-04.
   - "More on this verse ›" looks like a push but grows the sheet.
   - The Dictionary pushes full-screen from a Search sheet.
   - **Design:** one sheet-anatomy frame that every sheet follows.
4. **A tap on a verse means three things.** It selects in the reader, jumps the audio in the full player, and pushes
   Understand in the guide text. Proposal: drop passage text from the guide (NA-04 already does); in the full player a
   tap selects and offers "Play from".
5. **Translation is three taps away.** "BSB ⌄" opens Contents, then the Translation row, then the picker. MT-02
   (downloads) and LT-01 (online) are two versions of one picker. Home lists the OT first and Contents the NT first.
6. **Licensed versions and audio contradict each other.** LT-02 says the NKJV has no Listen; EG-03 and NT-05 play the
   BSB over it. Pick one.
7. **Two-sheet limit.** The full player's Guide opens a sheet over a full-screen modal, then verse → key word → peek,
   which makes three layers. Guide should first close the player to the mini player.
8. **Search lives only on Home.** Put a search field at the top of Contents. Dictionary browse is reachable only from
   Search's empty state.
9. **Plans lose context.** A plan chapter shows "‹ Home", not "‹ M'Cheyne". Home's plan line knows only the John plan.
   Two active plans are undefined.
10. **People and places have two doors that disagree.** The DI-04 dictionary entry and the TD/PD profile pages differ.
    Design one page per person or place, with the definition and the profile as sections.
11. **Compare** is an unlabelled glyph at default size and seems to vanish at AX5.
12. **The paused-reminders notice** (NT-09) shows only on Home, so readers arriving from a widget, notification or
    Spotlight never see it.

There are no true dead ends.

## 4. Haptics

**In the app now:**
- copy succeeded (success);
- verse tap (selection);
- bookmark toggled (selection);
- the sheet settles at a new detent (rigid);
- an original-language word tapped (light).

**Bugs:**
- The detent tick fires when a sheet opens large (Contents, Search, Translations, and every sheet at AX sizes).
  It should tick only after a drag by the reader (`components/Sheet.tsx:111-115`).
- The word tap has a haptic in the flowing layout but none in the AX3+ row layout (`OriginalWords.tsx:81`).

**Add:**
- Crossing a chapter on swipe (SW-02): one light impact at the one-third commit point. None on settle or spring-back.
- Download finished, while the reader is still on the sheet: success.
- Warning, only right after the reader's own action: a download failing on the tapped row, a failed Try again, a
  fourth Compare pick.

**Leave out:**
- play and pause;
- verse and chapter auto-advance;
- a passage finished;
- reminder time (the picker ticks itself);
- native menus.

Never during playback or follow-scroll. With VoiceOver on, keep only the confirmations.

## 6. Slop pass: fixed on the canvas in this round

- The `.big` icon rule hit evidence claims (J01-05 set one word per line and overlapped); it now applies to audio
  buttons only.
- References break at the space after the book, never inside "14:34" (NA-03/04, PG-07–09, J01, CP-04, VS-03/04).
- Curly apostrophes, en-dash ranges, and "1 more change" kept together.
- "Verse 5 of 51" as one phrase at every size; the AX5 player title takes two lines; the voice-info line wraps at AX5.
- The named back chip is a pill inside the sheet (DN-06), and sheet feet clear the home indicator (PG-04).
- Home everywhere follows IA-14 (one "Your plan" line), and the ⋯ menu reads Recent · Reading plans · Settings.
- VoiceOver's previous chapter from John 1 is Luke 24.
- "Translations" replaces the bare "Choose" pill.
- Copy fixes:
  - "Where we are" and "People and places" used consistently;
  - no repeated "Other passages that develop it" or "· BSB";
  - the WEB recording note sits on its own row;
  - the grammar of GS-03's speaker note;
  - "In plain words" in DN-02.

Left as listed: smaller nits in the reviewer reports (map labels, Search bolding, chevrons on plan rows, LORD small caps
in UI text, LT-05's share preview at AX5, MC-02's popover at AX5).
