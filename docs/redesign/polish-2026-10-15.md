# System polish, 15 Oct 2026

John asked for "proper UI polish … less like slop and a bit more modern", shared 13 YouVersion screenshots, and
said "i think we go white". The change lives in one block at the end of `.claude/skills/design/scripts/style-v2.css`,
so every v2 frame on the canvas picked it up (all 318 republished; each was checked against the live canvas first).

## What changed

| Area | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | warm chalk #F7F8F0, green-tinted greys | white #FFFFFF, neutral greys; dark #0B0C0B | John; YouVersion and Apple Books read cleaner on white |
| Muted text | #526D63 | #6B6E6C (5.1:1 on white); dark #9C9F9B (7.6:1) | contrast in both themes |
| Margins | 24 pt | 20 pt sides, 40 pt between sections | YouVersion's "Videos" / "See also" rhythm |
| Section titles | 13 pt grey labels | 22 pt bold sans in ink (20 pt in sheets) | the title carries the hierarchy, not a coloured label |
| Page titles | Literata serif | SF bold, tight tracking | serif is kept for Scripture, passage titles and book names |
| Rows | 52 pt, 1 px rule | 56 pt, hairline | more air |
| Controls | glass with shadow | white circles with a hairline ring; grey-fill pills | YouVersion's ×, "Close", "Find plans" |
| Sheets | inset, rounded all round | full width at the bottom, 36 pt top radius | the iOS default |
| Pictures | none | inset, 24 pt radius, the verse set over a soft dark fade, credit beneath | YouVersion's verse of the day |
