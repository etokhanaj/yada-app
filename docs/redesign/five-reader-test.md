# Five-reader test: the John passage guide

Run this once the John batch is generated and John has approved at least passages 1–3. Five sessions of
20 minutes each. Five people find most of the problems a screen has, so five is enough; more would mostly
repeat them. This test checks whether ordinary readers understand the passage, not whether they like the app.

## Who

Five adults who read the Bible at least now and then and have never studied Greek or Hebrew. At least two
should be new or returning readers, and at least one should use Larger Text or VoiceOver. Nobody who works on
Yada, and nobody who has seen the canvas.

## Setup

- TestFlight build with the John guide, on the tester's own phone if possible, at their own text size.
- Fresh install, so the first-launch question (ON-01) appears.
- One observer, who takes notes and doesn't help. If the tester asks "what do I do?", answer "What would you try?"
- Record what they say, not their face. Ask permission first, and delete the recordings after the write-up.

## Tasks (read them aloud, one at a time)

1. "You've just installed this. Start wherever you'd like." Watch which door they pick and how long they take.
2. "Read the first part of John and tell me in your own words what it is about." Don't prompt them.
3. "Is there anything here that Christians read differently? What are the options?"
4. "Pick one thing the guide says. How do you know it's true?" (They should reach the evidence.)
5. "Go back to reading where you were." Then: "Close the app and open it again. Where are you?"

## Questions afterwards

- What was the hardest word or sentence? (Write down the exact words.)
- Was anything on the screen there for no reason?
- Did you trust what it said? Why or why not?
- Would you open it again tomorrow? What for?

## Pass marks

| Measure | Pass |
|---|---|
| Picks a door within 10 seconds (task 1) | 4 of 5 |
| Retells 1:1–18 correctly in plain words (task 2) | 4 of 5 |
| Names both readings at a debated point without being shown (task 3) | 3 of 5 |
| Reaches the evidence for a claim (task 4) | 4 of 5 |
| Gets back to the right place (task 5) | 5 of 5 |
| Words called "hard" | none twice |

Any word named hard by two testers goes into the writing rules for the next prompt version. Any task that
fails its pass mark becomes a design issue on the canvas before more books are generated.

## Write-up

One page in `docs/redesign/`: who took part (no names), what each did on each task, the hard words verbatim,
and the three changes that matter most.
