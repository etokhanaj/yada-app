## Version 1.0

**Promotional text** (170; can change without a new build)

> Read the Bible with the original Greek and Hebrew beside it. See every word, what it means and where else it appears, all offline.

**Description** (4000)

> Yada is a Bible reader that shows you what the original Greek and Hebrew say.
>
> READ
> All 66 books in four built-in English translations: the Berean Standard Bible, World English Bible, King James Version and Literal Standard Version. Yada opens where you left off, at the verse you were reading. Adjust the text size, or use light and dark appearance.
>
> SEE THE ORIGINAL
> Tap Original to see a verse word by word in Greek or Hebrew, each word with its English gloss. Tap a word for its dictionary form, its parts and every other place it appears in the Bible.
>
> UNDERSTAND
> For selected verses, Understand explains what is happening in the passage, what the text plainly shows, and where readers differ. Every claim opens to the evidence behind it. Each explanation is checked by a person before it ships.
>
> COMPARE
> Set translations side by side to see where they agree and where they differ.
>
> SEARCH
> Find a reference ("jn 3 16"), an English word, or a Greek or Hebrew word across the whole Bible.
>
> MORE TRANSLATIONS
> Download the American Standard Version, Young's Literal Translation or the Majority Standard Bible, then read and search them offline.
>
> START HERE
> Read John passage by passage, in order.
>
> No account, and no personal data collected. Everything built in works without a connection. Sources lists every text and licence Yada uses.

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** Reading position, bookmarks, thread progress and reading settings stay
on the phone and in the reader's own iCloud (key-value storage, for backup and their other
devices); Yada has no server that receives them and cannot read them. The only network requests
Yada makes fetch the translations manifest and files from R2, carrying no user data.
*(iCloud sentence added 11 Oct 2026 with the icloud-sync branch: for John to approve, and to
carry into the Privacy Policy when it is written.)*
A **Privacy Policy URL** is still required before submission and before external TestFlight.
