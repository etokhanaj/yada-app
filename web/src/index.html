Start with John, and always know what comes next

Yada starts you in the Gospel of John, 46 passages long, and opens where you stopped. Progress counts the passages you have read, not days in a row, so a missed week loses nothing.

  1. Each passage opens with the point, in plain words

    Tap a passage’s title for its guide. It starts with what the passage says, in a few short lines, before you read a word of it.

  2. Then every verse, in everyday English

    The plain words come from unfoldingWord’s Simplified Text. Words it adds to make the meaning clear show in grey, so you can see what is not in the original.

  3. Tap a verse to understand it

    The verse opens in plain words, with the Greek or Hebrew, Compare, study notes and key words a tap below.

  4. See what the text itself shows

    Notice points out patterns counted from the text: John uses the word for “world” 78 times, more than any other book of the Bible. Where translations or readers differ, Yada says so.

Questions

Does Yada cost anything?

No. Yada is free, with no subscription. Reading, the explanations, the Greek and Hebrew, and listening are all included.

How is Yada different from other Bible apps?

Most Bible apps help you read more: plans, devotionals and videos. Study apps go deep but cost money and take time to learn. Yada explains the passage in front of you, in plain words, names the source of every line, and shows where Christians read it differently. It is free and has no ads.

Where do the explanations come from?

From named, openly licensed sources, never from AI. The plain-words lines are unfoldingWord’s Simplified Text, the notes on where readers differ come from unfoldingWord’s Translation Notes, and the study notes and profiles come from Tyndale House’s Open Study Notes. Cross-references come from OpenBible.info.

Sources, in the app, lists each one with its licence.

What does Yada know about me?

Nothing. There is no account and no analytics. Your reading place, bookmarks and settings are stored on your iPhone, and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We cannot see either.

The privacy policy has the details.

How do I get Yada?

Yada is in beta on Apple’s TestFlight, for iPhone. Open the invitation on your iPhone to install it.
