The Bible, explained one passage at a time

Every day starts on Today

Each morning opens with the day’s psalms, a line from them on a photograph, and Spurgeon’s Morning and Evening for the date. After 5 pm, the evening’s psalms lead.

A lamp keeps your streak. A day counts when you read or listen to Scripture. One day of rest a week never breaks it. If it stops, it starts again the next day you read.

Questions

Does Yada cost anything?

No. Yada is free, with no subscription. Reading, the explanations, the Greek and Hebrew, and listening are all included.

How is Yada different from other Bible apps?

Most Bible apps help you read more: plans, devotionals and videos. Study apps go deep but cost money and take time to learn. Yada explains the passage in front of you, in plain words, names the source of every line, and shows where Christians read it differently. It is free and has no ads.

Where do the explanations come from?

From named, openly licensed sources, never from AI. The plain-words lines are unfoldingWord’s Simplified Text, the notes on where readers differ come from unfoldingWord’s Translation Notes, and the study notes and profiles come from Tyndale House’s Open Study Notes. Cross-references come from OpenBible.info.

Sources, in the app, lists each one with its licence.

What does Yada know about me?

Nothing about your reading. There is no account and no analytics. Your reading place, highlights, notes and settings are stored on your iPhone, and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. We cannot see either. A prayer request you post is seen by other Yada users.

The privacy policy has the details.

How do I get Yada?

Yada is free on the App Store, for iPhone and Apple Watch. There are no ads and no subscription.
