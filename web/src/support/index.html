Common questions

Something in an explanation looks wrong

Email us the verse and what you noticed. Yada’s explanations come from named sources, and we would rather hear about a mistake than leave it in.

A download did not finish

Check your connection, then try again: open the Translations sheet and tap Download, or, for a book’s explanations and study notes, open the book again and tap Try again.

Moving to a new iPhone

If you are signed in to iCloud, your reading place, highlights, notes, Saved passages and settings follow you. If you are not, they stay on the old iPhone.

Report a prayer request

Tap Report on the request. A request reported by several people is hidden until we review it. You can also block the person who posted it, or email us.

Remove my prayer requests and circles

Delete a single request in Pray. To remove everything at once, open Home, then ⋯, then Remove my data: it deletes your requests, Prayed marks, reports and the circles you own.

The text is too small

Tap Aa in the reader to change the text size. Yada also follows Larger Text in the Settings app, under Accessibility.
