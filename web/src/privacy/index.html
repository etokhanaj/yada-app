Privacy policy

Yada has no account, analytics or advertising. Your reading stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Prayer requests you post are seen by other Yada users.

Who we are

Yada Bible Reader (“Yada”) is a free iPhone app, with an Apple Watch app and widgets, made by Creotok LTD (“we”, “us”), a company registered in England and Wales. You can reach us at creotok@gmail.com.

Your reading

Your reading place, highlights, notes, Saved passages and settings are kept on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so they follow you to another iPhone. We cannot read either.

The Apple Watch app and the widgets read the same data on your devices. Deleting the app deletes it from your iPhone; you can turn iCloud off for Yada in the Settings app.

Pray

A prayer request you post publicly is stored in Apple’s CloudKit public database for Yada and is visible to other Yada users, with a first name if you choose to add one. Prayed marks and reports are stored there too.

CloudKit links these records to an anonymous iCloud user ID. We never see your Apple ID, name or email address.

A circle is stored in its owner’s iCloud and shared through iCloud sharing with the people invited; its members see its requests.

A request reported by several people is hidden and reviewed by us, and we may remove it. You can delete your own requests in the app. Settings › Remove my data deletes your requests, your Prayed and report records, and the circles you own.

Pray together

Pray together uses SharePlay over your FaceTime call; Yada has no server in it. If you add a planned time to your calendar, Yada hands it to the system calendar sheet and does not read your calendar.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. When someone prays for your request, or a request you prayed for is answered, Apple delivers the notice through CloudKit. We use no other push service.

Photos

Yada can only add to your photo library, to save a share image you make. It never reads your photos.

What goes over the network

Bible texts, audio and pictures are downloaded from content.yadabible.com, hosted on Cloudflare R2. The requests carry no account, identifier or reading data; like any web host, Cloudflare sees the IP address of each request.

Licensed translations, where offered, are fetched through our server from API.Bible. The request carries only the passage reference.

What Yada does not do

  • It has no Yada account, no subscription and no advertising.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. Ask us and we will delete them.

Children

Yada is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date.
